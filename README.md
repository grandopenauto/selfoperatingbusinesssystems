# Self-Operating Business Systems
**Website:** https://selfoperatingbusinesssystems.com/  
**By:** Highest Degree Priorities (HDP)

A distinct, focused promotional site for bounded workflow automation, including examples for leads, service operations, and internal approvals. It is intentionally different from:
- https://sisobs.com/ — super-intelligent systems umbrella / visionary marketing front door.
- https://selfoperatedbusinesssystems.com/ — owner-directed control and educational guides.
- https://hdpjoin.com/ — the HDP network and broader business systems ecosystem.

## Architecture
Static HTML/CSS, progressive vanilla JavaScript. No backend, tokens, cookies, hidden analytics, customer information, or third-party JS code. The interactive illustrations are client-side only and are explicitly labeled conceptual. Contact opens an email draft.

## Publishing
Keep the existing root `CNAME` unchanged: `selfoperatingbusinesssystems.com`.
Use GitHub Pages main/root and wait for custom-domain HTTPS certificate provisioning.
Verify desktop/mobile, keyboard nav, 3 scenario tabs, FAQs, links, SVG assets, HTTPS canonical, sitemap, and page 200 response. Submit sitemap in Search Console once verified.

## Content scope
“Self-operating” describes permissioned, bounded progression of business work—not a claim of an unattended fully autonomous business or guaranteed customer outcomes.
